SUPPORT

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with BIT Solution.

What services do you offer? +
We offer Managed IT Services, Cloud Solutions, Cyber Security, Business VOIP Phone Services, Office Network Installation, Digital Marketing, Security Camera Installation, Procurement Services, IT Consulting, and 24/7 IT Support — everything your business needs under one roof.
How fast do you respond to support requests? +
Within minutes. We provide 24/7 support with guaranteed response times backed by an enterprise ticketing system. Critical issues are escalated immediately to our senior engineers.
Do you work with small businesses? +
Absolutely. Our solutions are scalable and priced for businesses of all sizes — from 5-person startups to 500-employee enterprises. We tailor every plan to your specific needs and budget.
Are there long-term contracts? +
No lock-in required. We offer flexible monthly, quarterly, or annual plans. We earn your business every month through results, not contracts.
How do you secure our cloud data? +
Through a multi-layered approach: end-to-end encryption at rest and in transit, multi-factor authentication, role-based access controls, regular third-party audits, and 24/7 threat monitoring. Our cloud infrastructure is hosted on Australian data centres.
Where are your offices located? +
Our headquarters is in Sydney, NSW at 8 Naroon Drive, Kalkallo. We also have offices in Melbourne (123 Collins St), Brisbane (56 Edward St), and Perth (88 Hay St). On-site service is available at all locations.
